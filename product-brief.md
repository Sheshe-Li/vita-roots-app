# Product Brief — Vita Roots Signal Harvester

## User
Families using Vita Roots who have active meal plans, grocery lists, and supplement guides generated by the AI wellness agent. Specifically: the primary account holder managing the family's nutrition and wellness decisions, who may not have time to monitor medical research or commodity price shifts independently.

## Problem
A family's personalized wellness plan is a snapshot in time. Two things constantly invalidate it without the family knowing:

1. **Medical research moves.** New peer-reviewed findings in nutrition science may directly affect a member's current supplement protocol or meal plan — especially for members with health conditions, specific dietary philosophies, or life stages (pregnant, elderly, pediatric). Families have no way to know when research relevant to *their specific profile* has been published.

2. **Markets move.** Ingredients and supplements that families regularly purchase are subject to price volatility and supply disruption. A family budgeted at $150/week has no early warning when key items in their active grocery or supplement list are projected to increase significantly — leaving them reactive instead of proactive.

Both problems share the same failure mode: the plan becomes stale and the family doesn't know it.

## MVP
A signal harvesting layer that runs on top of the existing Vita Roots wellness agent and:

- **Catches** new PubMed/arXiv research publications and commodity price signals for items in active family plans
- **Enriches** each signal against the specific family member profiles it may affect
- **Separates** high-signal findings (peer-reviewed, adequate sample size, directly relevant) from noise (preprints, animal studies, unrelated conditions)
- **Compounds** into two human-in-the-loop decision flows:
  - Research alert: adopt new direction and regenerate affected plan, or flag and revisit later
  - Market alert: stock up early, substitute item, or stay the course

The MVP does not replace the wellness agent — it feeds it. The agent's existing `generate_meal_plan`, `generate_supplement_guide`, and `generate_grocery_list` methods are the compound layer.

## Success Metric
- A qualifying research publication triggers a family alert within 4 hours of indexing on PubMed
- A projected price increase ≥15% on an active grocery or supplement item triggers a family alert within 24 hours
- End-to-end demo (signal detected → enriched → filtered → decision presented to user) completes in under 5 minutes
- Phoenix Arize traces capture every signal event with `wellness.request_type`, `family.id`, and `signal.type` attributes
